# Client testimonial drafts — approval required before publication

These are **draft prompts only**, not actual client statements and not approved endorsements. Do not publish them as quotes or attribute them to named people unless the organisation's authorised representative approves the exact wording in writing. The live website therefore uses factual engagement summaries rather than fabricated first-person testimonials.

## Suggested quote request / draft direction

- **Owenico Consult** — If accurate, comment on the value of business support, operational structure and clearer day-to-day processes.
- **AirPockets Global** — If accurate, comment on system documentation, SOPs, revenue operations development and ongoing support.
- **Parallex Bank** — If authorised, comment on the value and relevance of market leadership or customer acquisition support. Do not disclose confidential strategy or performance data.
- **Spectrum Microfinance Bank** — Confirm the specific engagement scope first, then request an approved statement from the authorised representative.
- **Stellite Energy** — If accurate, comment on collaboration around logistics/fuel delivery application requirements and business process considerations.
- **ThriveTech Africa** — If accurate, comment on business development and setup support.
- **Kesley Green Farms** — If accurate, comment on accounting, financial analysis and business support.
- **SWAP Technologies Limited** — If accurate, comment on HR outsourcing and people-operations support.

## Approval checklist

1. Confirm the organisation name and exact scope of work.
2. Ask an authorised client representative to supply or approve the final first-person wording.
3. Obtain written approval to publish the quote, name, logo, role/title and any photo.
4. Verify every outcome or metric and obtain approval to disclose it.
5. Keep a dated approval record.

Suggested request: “Would you be comfortable providing or approving a short testimonial (2–3 sentences) describing the work GoldBuilders supported, what was useful, and any outcome you are happy to share publicly? We will send the final wording for your approval before publication.”
