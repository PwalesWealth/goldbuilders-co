# Booking Integration Next Steps

The form accepts a preferred date and WAT time window, stores the preference with the enquiry and includes it in the team notification. It does **not** inspect calendar availability or automatically reserve a time.

For live slots, calendar sync and automated reminders, choose a provider (such as Calendly, Cal.com or a Google Calendar appointment schedule), configure team availability and confirmation rules, and supply the public booking URL or embed. No booking URL has been guessed or represented as connected.
