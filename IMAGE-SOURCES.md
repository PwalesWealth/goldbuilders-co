# Image sources and usage notes

The website uses a small number of remote Pexels stock images as decorative, darkened hero backgrounds. These are served from Pexels image CDN and require an internet connection from the visitor's browser.

- Team discussion image: https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-group-of-people-having-a-meeting-in-the-office-7652055/ (photo by Thirdman)
- Workshop/team image: https://www.pexels.com/photo/colleagues-having-a-business-meeting-6340639/ (photo by Pavel Danilyuk)
- Analytics dashboard image: https://www.pexels.com/photo/close-up-shot-of-a-laptop-computer-12969403/ (photo by Atlantic Ambience)

Pexels states that photos and videos can be used for free in commercial and non-commercial projects without attribution, subject to its license restrictions. See https://www.pexels.com/legal-pages/license/ and review the current license before publishing. These images are atmospheric illustrations, not endorsements by the people depicted.
