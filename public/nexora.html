How does the 5 videos per week content engine work?

We script, edit, and post 5 high-converting short-form videos per week (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) designed to highlight your services, client proof, and drive traffic directly to your smart web intake form.

How does the smart website connect to the CRM?

When a client books an appointment or fills out an intake form on your website, their details automatically sync to your CRM dashboard. Your team is immediately notified, an automated confirmation email/SMS is sent, and an invoice can be generated automatically.

Who owns the accounts and software created?

You own 100% of all assets. Everything is registered under your business name. Upon project completion, we transfer all master login credentials via a secure password manager.
