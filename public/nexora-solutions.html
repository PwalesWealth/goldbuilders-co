Before you proceed

A few practical notes.

Are the displayed fees final?

No. These are proposed starting fees for initial planning. We confirm the exact scope and send a written proposal before work begins.

Can I combine several services?

Yes. Select multiple service cards to share a combined enquiry. One-off project fees and monthly retainers appear separately so they are not confused as a single payment period.

What if my need is not listed?

Select “Custom scope & implementation” in the relevant area. We will use the consultation to scope a suitable approach and quote.

Does selecting a service book or charge me?

No. Selection only prepares an enquiry. It does not create a booking, invoice, contract or payment obligation.
