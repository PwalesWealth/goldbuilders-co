  • Shared talent and leadership capability

  • Portfolio-level brand and communications leverage
  • New products created from shared intelligence

YIELD Global Impact Network

Leadership, community and marketplace initiatives.

The YIELD network complements the commercial and technology ventures with youth empowerment, fellowship, learning, community and mission pathways.

Youth Initiative Empowerment and Leadership Development

YIELD Global Impact

YIELD Global Impact is a youth empowerment, leadership development and ecosystem activation platform. Its long-term ambition is to reach one billion young people through one million transformation leaders.

Leadership · Fellowship · Activation

YIELD Global Fellowship

YIELD Global Fellowship is the fellowship pathway within YIELD Global Impact. It connects leadership formation with community, peer learning and practical activation, including the LeaderSHIFT Tour and related gatherings.

Learning · Capability · Social Impact

School of Impact

School of Impact is a learning and action platform in the YIELD Global Impact network, designed to connect personal capability, leadership development and purposeful community contribution.

Youth Community · Leadership · Opportunity

Inspire Generation NEXT · NEXT Community

Inspire Generation NEXT and NEXT Community are youth-facing community pathways associated with YIELD Global Impact. They connect young people with leadership development, peer networks, purposeful experiences and opportunities to contribute.

Lifestyle · Community · Fitness · Food · Travel

The Driven Life · 10X Community

The Driven Life / 10X Community of BeMore Coaching Company fosters continuous 10X growth in personal and professional development through webinars, workshops, meetups, fitness, healthy living, hangouts and trips.

Community Care · Social Support · Collaboration

WeCare Foundation

WeCare Foundation is the community-care initiative in the YIELD Global Impact network. It provides a platform for care-oriented initiatives and collaboration around community needs, with each programme defined by its scope, partners and participation criteria.

Faith · Finance · Friendship · Marketplace Impact

Marketplace Ministry · NEXT Missions

A praying community for spiritual growth through the Word and prayer, marketplace intercession, the Friday Weekly Prayer Series, 90 Days Moment of Change, 365 Days of Glory and Missionaries Transformation Week.
