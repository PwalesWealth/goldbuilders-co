BeMore Signature Methodology · 12-Month Journey

THE ARIS 10X FRAMEWORK

A structured high-performance framework designed to help you make extraordinary progress across the areas that shape your life, work and contribution — with a 10X growth ambition over 12 months.

Become 10X More in 12 Months. Not through motivation alone, but through intentional goals, capability-building, consistent execution, measurement and review.

A · Affluence

Build your economic capacity.

Strengthen your relationship with money, grow income-generating capability and make wiser financial decisions.

  • Financial intelligence and wealth habits
  • Income growth and opportunity creation
  • Economic capacity and resource management
R · Relevance

Increase your value.

Develop the competence, skills, credibility and positioning that make your contribution valuable in your chosen field.

  • Skills and professional capability
  • Personal brand and market positioning
  • Continuous learning and value creation
I · Influence

Expand your impact.

Grow your capacity to lead, build meaningful relationships, communicate ideas and create positive change.

  • Leadership and communication
  • Relationships, networks and collaboration
  • Visibility, service and measurable contribution
S · Sustenance

Build the capacity to last.

Develop the habits, systems, health and personal structures that help you sustain progress and keep growing.

  • Discipline, consistency and execution systems
  • Health, wellbeing and energy management
  • Resilience, reflection and long-term growth
The 12-Month Growth Cycle

Four quarters. Four dimensions. One intentional growth journey.

Q1 · Jan–Mar

Affluence

Strengthen economic capacity, financial habits and income opportunities.

Q2 · Apr–Jun

Relevance

Build skills, competence, credibility and professional value.

Q3 · Jul–Sep

Influence

Expand leadership, relationships, visibility and contribution.

Q4 · Oct–Dec

Sustenance

Consolidate habits, systems, wellbeing and sustainable performance.

How the framework is applied

Participants establish a baseline, define meaningful 12-month outcomes, translate each quarter's focus into practical goals and weekly actions, and review progress against agreed measures. The 10X ambition is adapted to each person's starting point and context; it is a growth target, not a guaranteed result.

What We Help You Solve

From fragmented effort to coordinated execution.

We begin with the operating context rather than a pre-packaged answer. The engagement can be scoped as a focused diagnostic, a time-bound initiative or a broader transformation programme—depending on readiness, risk and available capacity.

Career and professional growth

Leadership and people performance

Business and founder development

  • Business model and capability questions

Our Working Approach

A practical path from diagnosis to delivery.

  1. Discover: confirm priorities, stakeholders, current-state evidence and constraints.
  2. Design: agree the target outcomes, workstreams, governance, measures and scope.
  3. Deliver: co-create and implement the agreed systems, interventions and capability.
  4. Review: track progress, identify adjustments and transfer ownership to the client team.

Designed for use—not shelfware

Outputs are shaped around what leaders and delivery teams must decide, change, operate and measure. Scope, timeline, responsibilities and commercial terms are agreed before delivery starts.

  • Clear decision rights and ownership
  • Practical deliverables and implementation sequence
  • Measures and review cadence aligned to objectives
  • Knowledge transfer for sustainable internal adoption
