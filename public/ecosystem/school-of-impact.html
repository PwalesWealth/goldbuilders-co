Learning · Capability · Social Impact

Learn with purpose. Turn capability into impact.

School of Impact is a learning and action platform in the YIELD Global Impact network, designed to connect personal capability, leadership development and purposeful community contribution.

YIELD Global Impact NetworkLeadership & Community
Who This Serves

School of Impact

Young leaders, project teams, social innovators, volunteers and organisations building capability for meaningful community outcomes.

Programme pathway
Start with the need, define the experience and coordinate the people and partners required for responsible delivery.
Focus Areas

How this initiative creates room for action.

A clear programme proposition helps participants understand the purpose, participation path and kind of contribution that is being invited.

Practical learning

Structure learning around real situations and the capabilities participants need.

Action orientation

Encourage participants to apply what they learn through projects, service or community initiatives.

Impact reflection

Support review of lessons, participation and the next actions required.

Offer Architecture

Ways to participate

  • Learning pathways for leadership and impact capability
  • Workshops, guided learning and project-based experiences
  • Opportunities to connect learning with community activity
  • Partnership pathways for facilitators and organisations
Participation Pathway

From interest to meaningful participation.

The pathway keeps expectations clear and helps the programme team organise participants, partners and delivery activity.

  1. Step 1. Identify the capability or impact challenge
  2. Step 2. Select a relevant learning pathway
  3. Step 3. Apply learning through a defined action or project
  4. Step 4. Reflect on learning and plan next steps
