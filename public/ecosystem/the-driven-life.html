10X
Lifestyle · Community · Fitness · Food · Travel

A more intentional life, lived together.

The Driven Life Community, also known as the 10X Community, is a community of BeMore Coaching Company. It fosters continuous 10X growth across personal and professional development, with a focus on healthy living, high performance and productivity in life, business and career. Members connect through fitness, food, travel, relationships, learning and shared experiences.

The 10X Growth Experience

A community built for continuous growth.

Welcome to The Driven Life Community (also known as the 10X Community) of BeMore Coaching Company. The community cares about your performance and productivity in life, business and career, with healthy lifestyle and high performance at the centre.

Monthly Webinars & Weekly Workshops

Learn, reflect and build practical habits through regular learning and development sessions.

10X Meetups, Seminars & Conferences

Connect with people, ideas and conversations that support personal and professional development.

Hangouts & Trips

Build community through fitness, food, travel and shared lifestyle experiences announced by the team.

BeMore subscriber community

By joining the 10X Community, members become part of the BeMore subscriber community and gain access to most free training and resources. Specific activities, schedules, prices and access conditions are communicated with each announcement.

Participation Pathway

From interest to meaningful participation.

The pathway keeps expectations clear and helps the programme team organise participants, partners and delivery activity.

  1. Step 1. Follow announced experiences and activities
  2. Step 2. Review location, price and participation details
  3. Step 3. Register for an activity or express interest
  4. Step 4. Join the community and share future experience ideas
