YG
Youth Initiative Empowerment and Leadership Development

Develop leaders who multiply transformation.

YIELD Global Impact is a youth empowerment, leadership development and ecosystem activation platform. Its long-term ambition is to reach one billion young people through one million transformation leaders.

YIELD Global Impact NetworkLeadership & Community
Who This Serves

YIELD Global Impact

Young people, emerging leaders, campuses, communities, organisations, ministries, civic partners and collaborators working to expand leadership and opportunity.

Cross-Ecosystem Institution

GEI Trust

Global Epicentre Innovation Trust coordinates talent, ecosystems and innovation, and anchors LeaderSHIFT activation programmes connecting GoldBuilders Co. and YIELD Global Impact.

The billion-youth / one-million-leader statement is a long-term ambition, not a claim that the target has already been achieved. GEI Trust is the cross-ecosystem institution that anchors LeaderSHIFT; it is not a YIELD-owned initiative.
