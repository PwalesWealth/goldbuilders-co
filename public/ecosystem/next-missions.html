Faith · Finance · Friendship · Marketplace Impact

Faith that serves. Leadership that makes a difference.

The Marketplace Ministry Network / NEXT Missions is a praying community for people who desire to grow spiritually and build a relationship with God through His Son Jesus, with the help of the Holy Spirit, through the Word and prayer. It brings intercessors, Christian executives, entrepreneurs and professionals together to connect faith with work, leadership, prosperity, the prophetic and service in the marketplace.

Prayer & Spiritual Formation

A rhythm of prayer, growth and marketplace intercession.

The Marketplace Ministry is a community for growing up spiritually and for intercessors in the marketplace. Through the Word and prayer, members seek God, encourage one another and prepare to serve with faith and purpose.

Friday Weekly Prayer Series

Second Watch Hour

A weekly prayer series dedicated to intercession in the spiritual realm and standing in prayer for the marketplace before the enemy attempts to wreak havoc.

90 Days Moment of Change Midnight Prayers

Third Watch Hour · Annual October bootcamp

A 90-day journey of digital detox and spiritual growth, focused on transformation in seven key areas of life. The series includes midnight prayers and daily listening to messages from anointed ministers around the world.

365 Days of Glory Midnight Prayers

Fourth Watch Hour · 3:00 AM

A daily time to connect with God, seek guidance and experience spiritual awakening — to know Him and the power of His resurrection, and to be filled with the knowledge of His will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding.

Monthly Discipleship Training Bootcamp

Missionaries Transformation Week

A monthly discipleship bootcamp focused on foundational Christian doctrines and the basics of spiritual growth, with teachings and discussions that prepare participants for their walk of faith and missions around the world.

What the bootcamp includes

  • Daily faith-based teachings and activities
  • Foundational doctrine and practical Christian-growth discussions
  • Weekly projects and group fellowships
  • Learning about rights in Christ and how to discover and live out calling and purpose in God
  • Preparation for purposeful service and missions
Our marketplace conviction

The Marketplace Ministry exists to enforce the will of God on earth through prosperity and the prophetic, while encouraging spiritual maturity, prayer and purposeful service.
