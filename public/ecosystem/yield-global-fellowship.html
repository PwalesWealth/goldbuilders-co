YF
Leadership · Fellowship · Activation

Build leaders through fellowship, learning and action.

YIELD Global Fellowship is the fellowship pathway within YIELD Global Impact. It connects leadership formation with community, peer learning and practical activation, including the LeaderSHIFT Tour and related gatherings.

YIELD Global Impact NetworkLeadership & Community
Who This Serves

YIELD Global Fellowship

Young leaders, emerging professionals, campus leaders, community builders and collaborators seeking a purposeful leadership and service community.

Programme pathway
Start with the need, define the experience and coordinate the people and partners required for responsible delivery.
Focus Areas

How this initiative creates room for action.

A clear programme proposition helps participants understand the purpose, participation path and kind of contribution that is being invited.

Formation

Build reflective, responsible and purpose-led leadership habits.

Fellowship

Create space for relationships, peer learning, mentoring and collaboration.

Activation

Turn shared learning into local programmes, service and opportunities to lead.

Offer Architecture

Ways to participate

  • Fellowship and peer-community pathways
  • Leadership conversations and development experiences
  • Participation in relevant YIELD activations
  • Cross-community collaboration and partnership opportunities
Participation Pathway

From interest to meaningful participation.

The pathway keeps expectations clear and helps the programme team organise participants, partners and delivery activity.

  1. Step 1. Register interest and share your leadership context
  2. Step 2. Identify an appropriate fellowship or programme pathway
  3. Step 3. Participate in learning, connection and activation
  4. Step 4. Continue through peer accountability and service
