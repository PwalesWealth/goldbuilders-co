GoldBuilders Diagnostic

High-Performance Transformation Assessment

Assess the strength of your organization across seven connected dimensions. Your result generates a performance score, maturity tier and priority areas for discussion.

How It Works

A 7-dimension view of organizational performance.

Rate each dimension from 1 (early / weak) to 10 (strong / well established). The assessment produces a directional score, identifies your maturity level and highlights dimensions that deserve attention.

  • Strategy & Direction
  • People & Leadership
  • Revenue Operations
  • Finance & Controls
  • Technology & Data
  • Operations
  • Brand & Communication

What You Receive

Your result includes an overall High-Performance Index, maturity tier, dimension scores, priority recommendations and a next-step path with GoldBuilders Co.

Not a formal audit.
Use it as a strategic conversation starter, not as an assurance opinion or certification.
Assessment Form

Score Your Organization

Enter your own ratings from 1 to 10. Your progress updates as each dimension is completed.

Use your best current judgement. There are no “right” answers — the value comes from identifying where capability is weakest relative to your ambition.
